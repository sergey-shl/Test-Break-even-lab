The formula, brought to life
Break-even output = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost)

What does this model assume?

One product; a constant selling price and variable cost per cup; constant fixed costs within the relevant output range; all cups produced are sold. Changing price does not automatically change demand here. In reality, a higher price may reduce sales, and expanding output may require more staff or equipment.

Contribution per cup = price − variable cost per cup. Profit = total contribution − fixed costs. Margin of safety = sales output − exact break-even output. A negative margin indicates a sales shortfall. Minimum whole cups to avoid a loss are rounded up; the chart and margin use the exact break-even value.
